Point it at your own app

This page is a sandbox with seeded defects. The engine is UI-free and runs anywhere — same-origin policy means it must execute inside the page being audited, so here are the three ways to do that.

1. Coding-agent loop recommended

Give your AI coding agent a shell command. It audits your dev server, reads structured findings, fixes the source, and re-audits. Exit code gates CI too. 

npx dom-surgeon audit http://localhost:5173 --json --fail-on critical

2. Drop-in script

Add one line to your dev or staging build. The audit engine and the WebMCP tools register against your live DOM, so a browsing agent can audit your real app conversationally. 

<script src="ORIGIN/dom-surgeon-engine.js"></script>

3. Bookmarklet

Zero install. Drag this to your bookmarks bar, open any page you own, and click it — findings print to the console.

DOM-Surgeon: audit this page

Programmatic API once loaded: DOMSurgeon.audit(), DOMSurgeon.diff(baseline), DOMSurgeon.toMarkdown(result).
